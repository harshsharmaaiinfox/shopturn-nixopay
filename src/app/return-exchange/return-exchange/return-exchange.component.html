Return Policy

Easy returns for your complete satisfaction

At TURN LIFE RETAIL PRIVATE LIMITED (operating as shopturnlife.com), we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If, for any reason, you are not completely satisfied with a purchase, we invite you to review our policy on returns.

Order Cancellation Rights

You are entitled to cancel your order within 0-7 days without providing any reason for doing so. The deadline for cancelling an order is 0-7 days from the date on which you received the goods.

To cancel your order, you can:

  • Log in to your account and go to "My Orders"
  • Select the order you wish to cancel
  • Click on "Cancel Order" button
  • Or contact our customer support team at info@shopturnlife.com

Conditions for Returns

In order for the goods to be eligible for a return, please ensure that:

  • The goods were purchased within the last 0-7 days
  • The goods are in the original packaging with all tags attached
  • The goods are unused, unwashed, and in their original condition
  • All accessories, labels, and freebies (if any) are included
  • The product is not damaged due to misuse or negligence

Please note that items must be returned in their original condition to be eligible for a refund or exchange.

Return Process

To initiate a return:

  • Log in to your account on shopturnlife.com
  • Navigate to "My Orders" section
  • Select the order you want to return
  • Click on "Return" and select the reason for return
  • Follow the instructions to complete the return process
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging
  • Ship the item back using the provided return label or as instructed

Once we receive and verify the returned product, we will process your refund or exchange as per your request.

Goods That Cannot Be Returned

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Goods made to your specifications or clearly personalized
  • Goods that are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons and were unsealed after delivery
  • Goods that are, after delivery, inseparably mixed with other items
  • Items damaged due to misuse, negligence, or wear and tear
  • Items without original packaging, tags, or accessories
  • Items returned after the 7-day return period
  • Gift cards and promotional vouchers

Refund Processing

Once we receive and verify your returned product:

  • We will process your refund within 0-7 working days
  • The refund will be credited to your original payment method
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the refund is processed
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
