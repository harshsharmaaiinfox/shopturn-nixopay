Shipping Policy

Fast and reliable delivery to your doorstep

At TURN LIFE RETAIL PRIVATE LIMITED (operating as shopturnlife.com), we are committed to delivering your orders safely and on time. This policy outlines our shipping and delivery procedures to ensure a smooth shopping experience.

For Normal Orders

All normal orders will be delivered within 10-15 days from the date of order confirmation. The delivery time may vary depending on:

  • Your delivery location
  • Product availability
  • Weather conditions
  • Holidays and festivals
  • Any unforeseen circumstances

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS that you can use to track your order status in real-time.

For Bulk Orders

All bulk orders will be delivered within 10-15 business days from the date of order confirmation. Bulk orders require additional processing time due to:

  • Custom packaging requirements
  • Quality checks and verification
  • Special handling procedures
  • Coordination with logistics partners

For bulk orders, we recommend placing your order well in advance to ensure timely delivery. Our customer support team will keep you informed about the status of your bulk order throughout the process.

Shipping Locations

We currently ship to all major cities and towns across India. Delivery times may vary based on your location:

  • Metro Cities: 7-10 business days
  • Tier 2 & 3 Cities: 10-12 business days
  • Remote Areas: 12-15 business days

Please note that delivery to remote or hard-to-reach areas may take longer. We will make every effort to deliver your order as quickly as possible.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated based on:

  • Order value
  • Delivery location
  • Product weight and dimensions
  • Shipping method selected

Free shipping may be available on orders above a certain value. Please check our website for current offers and promotions.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive:

  • An email confirmation with tracking details
  • SMS notification with tracking number
  • Real-time updates on order status
  • Estimated delivery date

You can track your order by:

  • Logging into your account on shopturnlife.com
  • Going to "My Orders" section
  • Clicking on "Track Order" for the specific order
  • Or using the tracking number provided in your email/SMS

Delivery Issues

If you face any issues with delivery, please contact us immediately:

  • Delayed delivery beyond the estimated time
  • Damaged package upon receipt
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Missing items from your order
  • Unable to locate your delivery address

Our customer support team will assist you in resolving any delivery-related issues promptly.
