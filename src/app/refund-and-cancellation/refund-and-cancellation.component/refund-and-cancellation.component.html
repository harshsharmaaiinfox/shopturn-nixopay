Refund and Cancellation Policy

Understanding our refund and cancellation process

At TURN LIFE RETAIL PRIVATE LIMITED (operating as shopturnlife.com), we strive to ensure your complete satisfaction with every purchase. This policy outlines our refund and cancellation procedures to provide you with a transparent and hassle-free shopping experience.

Refund Policy

If any refunds are approved by the company, it will take 0-7 working days to credit the amount to your original source account. The refund process depends on various factors including the payment method used and the bank's processing time.

Refunds will be processed using the same payment method that was used for the original transaction. For example:

  • Credit/Debit Card payments will be refunded to the same card
  • UPI payments will be refunded to the same UPI account
  • Net Banking payments will be refunded to the same bank account
  • Wallet payments will be refunded to the same wallet

Please note that the refund amount will exclude any shipping charges, unless the return is due to a defect or error on our part.

Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your order before it is shipped. Once your order has been shipped, you cannot cancel it, but you can return it as per our Return Policy.

To cancel an order:

  • Log in to your account on shopturnlife.com
  • Go to "My Orders" section
  • Select the order you wish to cancel
  • Click on "Cancel Order" button
  • Alternatively, you can contact our customer support team

If your order has already been processed and shipped, the cancellation request will not be accepted. In such cases, you may return the product as per our Return Policy.

Refund Processing Time

Once we receive and verify the returned product, we will initiate the refund process. The timeline for refund processing is as follows:

  • Processing Time: 0-7 working days after approval
  • Credit Card/Debit Card: 3-7 business days after processing
  • UPI: 1-3 business days after processing
  • Net Banking: 3-7 business days after processing
  • Wallet: 1-2 business days after processing

Please note that the actual credit to your account may take additional time depending on your bank or payment gateway's processing time.

Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for refund:

  • Items damaged due to misuse or negligence
  • Items without original packaging and tags
  • Personalized or customized products
  • Items returned after the return period
  • Gift cards and vouchers
